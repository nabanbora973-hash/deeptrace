Face-swap forensic analysis

Three streams of evidence.
One explainable verdict.

DeepTrace inspects a video or image for the traces a face-swap cannot avoid: blending seams in the spatial domain, generator fingerprints in the frequency domain, and flicker / identity jitter / a missing blood-pulse in the temporal domain. Each stream is scored, reliability-weighted, and fused into a calibrated probability — with the arithmetic of that verdict shown to you.

How the verdict is produced

01 · spatial

Image-plane traces

Blending-seam energy, sensor-noise discontinuity across the face boundary, chroma mismatch and sharpness discontinuity — all measured as scale-free ratios on the aligned 256×256 crop.

02 · frequency

Generator fingerprint

High-frequency energy ratio, spectral power-law slope, periodic peak prominence (the classic GAN grid), angular concentration and 8×8 DCT block statistics from a Hann-windowed FFT.

03 · temporal

Temporal coherence

Per-frame evidence flicker, face-descriptor jitter between frames, and remote photoplethysmography — a real face carries a blood-pulse, generated skin does not.

04 · fusion

Reliability-weighted fusion

A short clip cannot support pulse evidence, so the temporal stream is down-weighted instead of trusted. Absence of evidence is never treated as evidence of authenticity.

What this honestly is — and is not

It measures real signals

Every stream computes genuine forensic statistics on your input, and every verdict is accompanied by the evidence map and the exact contributions. The evaluation harness reports AUC/EER with bootstrap confidence intervals.

Its calibration is the current weak link

The shipped decision layer is fitted on a small synthetic corpus (generated by demo/make_dataset.py), because no licensed deepfake dataset is bundled here. Refit with train/extract_features.py + train/fit_calibration.py on FaceForensics++ / Celeb-DF / DFDC before quoting accuracy on real attacks.

It is evidence, not proof

Artifact detectors can be defeated by strong adversarial post-processing, and a clean verdict does not establish authenticity. Provenance metadata (C2PA) and source verification remain the stronger evidence class.
